Our lab aims to address unmet clinical needs and develop cutting-edge imaging technologies by leveraging the full potential of artificial intelligence for advanced diagnostics. We are particularly dedicated to developing and validating innovative tools that enhance diagnostic accuracy and improve patient outcomes, particularly in diseases like cancer. One of our key achievements is the development of Fluorescence Imitating Brightfield Imaging (FIBI), a slide-free imaging technique that enables rapid, high-resolution tissue analysis directly in the operating room.

We collaborate with multidisciplinary teams, bringing together expertise from biomedical engineering, AI, and clinical research to drive innovative solutions in modern medicine.
